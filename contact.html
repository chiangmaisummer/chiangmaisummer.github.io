Directions

  • From CNX Airport: Use Grab/Taxi to “Prapokklao Rd Soi 6”. Enter the Old City via Suan Dok Gate or Chiang Phuak Gate depending on traffic; the lane is near Wat Phra Singh.
  • From Chiang Mai Railway Station: Take a taxi/Grab straight to the Old City → “Prapokklao Rd Soi 6”.
  • Landmark: Wat Phra Singh. Our lane (Soi 6) is a short walk from the temple area.
